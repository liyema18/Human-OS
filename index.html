ABOUT THE PROJECT

Knowledge is a human right.

Human OS is an independent learning project designed to make useful knowledge easier to find and understand.

How we approach knowledge

  • Explain complex ideas in plain language without stripping away important nuance.
  • Separate established evidence from uncertainty, interpretation and opinion.
  • Link readers to reputable sources so they can check and go deeper.
  • Respect different cultures, contexts and levels of prior knowledge.
  • For health, law and finance, provide education rather than personalised professional advice.

Offline and privacy

Bookmarks and reading history stay in your browser's local storage on this device. The service worker can cache the app shell and bundled library. News feeds require internet access and may not be cached.

This starter app does not create accounts or send bookmarks to a server.
